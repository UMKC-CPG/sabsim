# Submission guide — si_sio2

Si(100)/alpha-quartz SiO2(001) small demo, 50 eV Ar

Generated by `sabsim prepare` (DESIGN.md §10). Each script fills
the stage folder of the same name: deliverables beside this guide,
bulk output under `intermediate/` (§10.8). Submit the jobs in the
order below, checking each result before submitting the next
(§10.2). Each script also prints, on success, what to check and
the next job to submit. The two `.library` builds come first:
each makes its surface's environment library ONCE per recipe
(§3.5) and exits at once, successfully, when the library is
already there — so they may always be included in the chain.

## Step by step

1. `sbatch prep_surf1_si.library.slurm` (library_surf1; independent — submit in either order, or together)
2. `sbatch prep_surf2_sio2.library.slurm` (library_surf2; independent — submit in either order, or together)
3. `sbatch prep_surf1_si.slurm` (prep_surf1; after `prep_surf1_si.library.slurm` has finished and been checked)
4. `sbatch prep_surf2_sio2.slurm` (prep_surf2; after `prep_surf2_sio2.library.slurm` has finished and been checked)
5. `sbatch bond_si_sio2.slurm` (bond; after `prep_surf1_si.slurm` and `prep_surf2_sio2.slurm` have finished and been checked)
6. `sbatch analysis_si_sio2.slurm` (analysis; after `bond_si_sio2.slurm` has finished and been checked)

## Chained form, for a person who trusts the gates

Submit everything at once and let the scheduler hold each job
until the ones it depends on succeed (`afterok`):

```bash
library_surf1_id=$(sbatch --parsable prep_surf1_si.library.slurm)
library_surf2_id=$(sbatch --parsable prep_surf2_sio2.library.slurm)
prep_surf1_id=$(sbatch --parsable --dependency=afterok:$library_surf1_id prep_surf1_si.slurm)
prep_surf2_id=$(sbatch --parsable --dependency=afterok:$library_surf2_id prep_surf2_sio2.slurm)
bond_id=$(sbatch --parsable --dependency=afterok:$prep_surf1_id:$prep_surf2_id bond_si_sio2.slurm)
analysis_id=$(sbatch --parsable --dependency=afterok:$bond_id analysis_si_sio2.slurm)
```
